## Findings

1. **M2-001 — P1 — unsupported command meaning still discarded**
   - File and line: `services/api/app/nl_compiler.py:181`; related patterns at lines 49, 210 and 219.
   - Reproduction: POST `/api/workflows/compile` with “When a car enters Service Bay A, open its hood”; “When a car enters Service Bay A with its hood emitting smoke and open, create an alert”; or “When a car stays in Service Bay A for 5 minutes or 10 minutes, create an alert”.
   - Expected behavior: reject unsupported actuation, additional conditions, and ambiguous durations without a draft.
   - Actual behavior: all return HTTP 200, `ok=true`. Actuation becomes notification, smoke disappears, and only 300 seconds survives. Entry “for 5 minutes” also drops its duration. Global stripping and greedy visual regexes consume semantics never represented in the DSL.
   - Minimal suggested fix: validate complete supported clauses in their semantic positions; consume only the parsed condition/duration and recognized notification action. Reject remaining or ambiguous clauses and add API regressions.

2. **M2-003 — P1 — active transport and admission exceed the deadline**
   - File and line: `services/api/app/teacher.py:451`; indefinite join at line 470; guard wait at line 353 and `services/api/app/vlm_router.py:304`.
   - Reproduction: stream 64 KiB every 5 ms with a 60 ms overall timeout, 50 ms read timeout, and concurrency one. Leave it running after caller timeout and make another call. Separately hold admission for 150 ms with a 50 ms overall timeout.
   - Expected behavior: expiry cancels/closes transport, bounds response consumption and cleanup, and prevents late sends/confirmations.
   - Actual behavior: caller returns timeout at 60 ms, but its worker remains active/unclosed; 58 chunks/about 3.8 MB are consumed and the next call times out waiting for its permit. An endless compliant stream can occupy capacity and accumulate data indefinitely. Admission contention also blocks past expiry, then sends a POST and returns confirming `ok` at approximately 152 ms.
   - Minimal suggested fix: use owned cancellable streaming transport with a response-byte cap; enforce the deadline through admission, transfer and parsing; cancel/close expired requests and reject late sends/results. Preserve permits until bounded cleanup completes.

3. **M2-009 — P2 — completed shared results bypass cache bounds**
   - File and line: `services/api/app/vlm_router.py:376`.
   - Reproduction: ten distinct input/version combinations with cache capacity two.
   - Expected behavior: completed shared results are released or explicitly bounded.
   - Actual behavior: primary cache contains two entries/eight evictions; `_inflight_result` retains all ten indefinitely.
   - Minimal suggested fix: per-request result objects retained by active waiters, or bounded expiry. This previously documented P2 remains outside the authorized recovery implementation scope.
